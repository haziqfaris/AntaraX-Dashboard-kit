Where it opens

  • Help → Contact support opens Get help. Help → Send feedback opens Send feedback.
  • Help → Your requests lists the person's requests; Owners and Admins see their organisation's, and may reply and close but never change a request's status. A new reply shows as a count on Help.
  • Report this on an error screen opens Get help with the error reference filled in.
  • The Help centre's foot opens Get help.

What the product sends with it

The product, the organisation, the person and their role, the page, the version and any error reference. Screenshots and files are added by the person, by pasting, dropping or choosing them. The product keeps nothing of the request; YaraOne does.
