Benchmark

What to benchmark the standard against

No single product is the answer. Each layer has a clear leader, and each leader has gaps the standard must not copy.

Customer shell and controls

Angka, on Yara's frame

Angka has the fullest control set of any product: 11 of 13. Yara's frame is the one four products already share.

  • Angka: search with ⌘K, a global Add, Ask with the sparkle, a business switcher, light, dark and system themes, a REKA support-view banner, and nav counters with spoken labels
  • Angka phones: a drawer plus a bottom bar with a raised Add and a More sheet
  • Yara frame: sidebar 240, floating top bar with the AntaraX A and product pill, Help and User guide in the menu, an accessible drawer with a focus trap

Don't copy: Angka's floating sidebar look, plum focus ring, extra typefaces and missing credit line; Yara's "Powered by".

Customer dashboard

Yara, with Angka's tone

Yara has the most complete operational home. Angka shows how to say the same things in plain words.

  • Yara: what needs attention first; money tiles only for roles allowed to see money; a 7, 30, 90 days or all time picker; count tiles, charts and a time summary; a module slot
  • Janka and AX Frame have the same "act on these first" idea, which confirms the pattern
  • Angka: one plain sentence at the top ("September is 46% understood"), "Needs you", "Coming up"

Don't copy: money with a space ("RM 550,634.00"), numbers not in mono, tiles that overflow on phones.

AXLE console

Angka's content in AX Frame's look

Angka's AXLE is the broadest console, at 28 places, and antarax-api already names it the pattern. AX Frame's is the only one in the brand kit's dark AXLE look.

  • Angka: 28 places in Watch, Products, Run and Trail; five staff rungs that match AXLE Central's ladder; status strip, range picker, "needs an operator"; support view; announcements and the help editor
  • AX Frame: dark #071233 ground, REKA orange signal, the "AXLE · Product" name, an open-access banner, a "How this page works" explainer on every page
  • Yara and Janka: their overview tables (organisation readiness, data health, needs a human) are worth keeping

Don't copy: Angka's light plum theme; AX Frame's phone header bug and two-mode switch.
