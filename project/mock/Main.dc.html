DASHBOARD KIT · PROJECT TEMPLATE · v1.0

REKA projects: dashboards REKA builds for clients

A client's project carries the client's brand and never looks like an AntaraX product. It keeps the places people look for things (the shell's top bar, Help, the account) and the standards nobody sees (security, data, speed, accessibility). Everything that makes AntaraX look like AntaraX stays out.

Three dashboards

app/ AntaraX products Each product's own identity inside the AntaraX shell: the brand pill, the product's palette, "by AntaraX". Janka, Yara, AX Frame, Angka, Tinara, PLExyz Grid
project/ · new REKA projects The client's own brand on a neutral base. The shell's places and the standards beneath the look; nothing of AntaraX's look. Bespoke work REKA builds and runs for a client
axle/ AXLE REKA's staff console, the same for products and projects. Clients never see it. A project's AXLE is usually R8 Base with one organisation: the client
All three: tickets and feedback through YaraOne (front office in the client's look, back office in AXLE) · the engineering standards ("Built right") · the Never list's rules on data, waits, passwords and pop-ups · support sessions with the organisation's consent.

Kept from the shell (owner, 16 Oct 2026)

  • The top bar's order: the client's logo and the project's name, the page title, the project's slot, search, notifications, Help, then the account at the far right
  • The account menu's items and order; Help's menu: User guide, Help centre, Send feedback, Contact support, Your requests
  • The standard pages: /account, /settings, /guide, /help, the legal pages, and the organisation's admin area
  • The shared words and roles: Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer; Sign out, Organisation
  • Getting in, banners in order, notifications, dialogs, toasts and errors
  • Every wait animated, never "Loading…" alone or a spinner
  • Built right: passwords, forms, PDPA data handling, speed, security, accessibility

Never in a client project

  • The AntaraX name, the AntaraX A, the brand pill or a product chip
  • Poppins or JetBrains Mono; any colour from the AntaraX brand kit, including product palettes and REKA orange
  • The AI sparkle, the halo, the intan, BaTech, AntaraX's loaders
  • "by AntaraX", "Made with ‹Product›", plan names such as Seed or Nova
  • A floating, pill-shaped top bar or 20px card corners
  • REKA's legal line on the client's legal pages: those are the client's

The colour rule is checked by a script: no hex from the brand kit's tokens or product palettes may appear in a project's styles.

The framework (owner, 16 Oct 2026)

One stack for every project, so any REKA engineer or coding agent can pick one up:

  • Next.js (App Router), TypeScript strict, Tailwind CSS
  • shadcn/ui on Radix, re-themed to the project base; Lucide icons
  • TanStack Query and Table; React Hook Form with Zod
  • Supabase: Auth, Postgres with RLS, Storage
  • Refine on top, only for create-and-edit-heavy back offices
  • Vitest and Playwright
